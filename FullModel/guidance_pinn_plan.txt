PLAN: BUILDING THE GUIDANCE PINN
MSc thesis - PINN-based closed-loop guidance for powered descent to 433 Eros
Recorded 2026-09-16

Step by step, in order. Each block consumes the output of the one before it, so
the blocks are done in sequence and not in parallel: the dispersion that comes
out of one is the initial condition of the next.


================================================================================
1. LANDING SITE
================================================================================

STATUS: DECIDED.

  - The site in the pond area, the one already in use.

No further work in this block. It is listed because everything downstream is
defined relative to it, and because the multi-site work in block 5 has to come
back and relax this.


================================================================================
2. PARKING ORBIT
================================================================================

GOAL
  Select and fully justify the parking orbit, and establish the orbit
  determination error there, so that the state at the start of the braking
  manoeuvre is dispersed realistically rather than assumed.

STEPS
  - Select the parking orbit. Justify it from literature or from theory, not by
    assertion. The justification has to cover why this orbit and not the
    alternatives.
  - Establish the OD error achievable in that orbit. This replaces any assumed
    constant with something defensible.
  - Propagate that OD error to the epoch where the braking manoeuvre starts.

OUTPUT
  A well justified CLOUD OF POINTS from which the braking manoeuvre can begin.
  This cloud, not a single nominal point, is what the next block takes as input.

ALSO IN THIS BLOCK
  - Attempt LCA (linear covariance analysis) here, alongside the Monte Carlo, if
    it can be made to work.


================================================================================
3. BRAKING MANOEUVRE + TCM
================================================================================

GOAL
  Deliver a realistic dispersion for the descent initial condition, produced by
  a manoeuvre sequence that is designed the way a real one would be.

STEPS
  - Explain how the BURN TIME and BURN DIRECTION are obtained for the open-loop
    propagation. This is the nominal design.
  - Explain WHEN and WHERE the correction is applied, and WHY that epoch and
    that location. The choice has to be argued, not inherited.
  - Run an OD filter through the coast, so the correction is computed from an
    ESTIMATE of the state at the correction epoch, the way it would be on
    board - not from the true state.
  - Apply the correction, then propagate the TRUTH DYNAMICS to see where the
    spacecraft actually arrives.
  - Monte Carlo the whole sequence to get the dispersion of the descent initial
    condition.
  - Include THRUST MAGNITUDE and POINTING errors on both burns. Everything in
    this block is to be as realistic as it can be made.

OUTPUT
  The dispersed initial condition for the descent: the cloud of states at
  handover, with its covariance.

ALSO IN THIS BLOCK
  - Attempt LCA here as well, alongside the Monte Carlo, if it can be made to
    work.


================================================================================
4. SCvx BLOCK
================================================================================

STATUS: essentially fully implemented already. This block is tuning and
generation, not new development.

STEPS
  - Re-tune SCvx for the REAL dispersion and the REAL initial conditions coming
    out of block 3. The sampling envelope and the initial-condition
    distribution follow from the measured handover dispersion, not from a
    guess.
  - Generate the training data for the PINN on that basis.

OUTPUT
  The dataset the PINN trains on, matched to the dispersion the vehicle will
  actually see.


================================================================================
5. PINN BLOCK
================================================================================

GOAL
  Get the guidance network working, then generalise it across sites.

STEPS
  - Test the current architecture across many configurations: loss function,
    network size, data, and whatever else is worth varying.
  - If it works, and the results hold up in the FULL SIMULATION and not only in
    open loop, move to multi-site:
      - train a single PINN on at least two sites,
      - and if that works, keep increasing the number of sites.

OUTPUT
  The guidance PINN, and a statement of how far it generalises across sites.
