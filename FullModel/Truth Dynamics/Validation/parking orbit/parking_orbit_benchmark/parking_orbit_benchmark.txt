================================================================================
1. WHAT WAS DONE
================================================================================

The truth propagator's step size is selected on a parking orbit by STEP HALVING.
No reference trajectory is used: the arc is propagated at dt and again at dt/2,
and the difference between the two is taken as the error estimate at dt. For a
scheme of order p the finer run carries 2^-p of the coarser run's error, so their
difference is (1 - 2^-p) of it, 94% for RK4. The ladder costs nothing extra
because every propagation is the fine partner of the run above it and the coarse
partner of the run below.

SETUP
  Trajectory   circular orbit, radius 35 km, randomly oriented, seed 20260916
  Arc          1.5 revolutions; period 61560.0 s, so tf = 92339.9 s
  Frame        INERTIAL (see Validation/Frame Validation for why)
  Integrator   RK4, fixed step, 4 evaluations per step
  Gravity      Werner-Scheeres POLYHEDRAL, Gaskell ver64q, 49 152 facets,
               density set to reproduce GM = 4.4665e5 m^3/s^2
  Perturbations  none. No SRP, no third body, no thrust.

STEP SIZES TESTED
  1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2 s, walked coarse to fine.

  Every step is EXACTLY the size stated. The step is not rescaled to divide the
  arc evenly - a requested 1024 s step that silently becomes 1014.7 s is not the
  step that was asked for, and the halving ratio would then be 1.989 rather than
  2, putting the convergence against a slightly wrong abscissa. tf is not a
  multiple of these steps, so the propagator's termination condition trims only
  the FINAL step, the one that would overshoot the end of the arc.

  nearly identical numbers, so the last two rungs measure noise and not accuracy.
  Halving there doubles the cost and returns nothing.


================================================================================
3. SELECTION
================================================================================

  TOLERANCE      1e-5 m

  SELECTED       dt = 32 s      error 1.004e-06 m
                                11 544 gravity evaluations for 1.5 orbits
                                4.29 s wall clock

  32 s is the coarsest step on the ladder that meets the tolerance, and it meets
  it with a factor of 10 to spare. The next step up, 64 s, gives 1.702e-05 m and
  misses.

WHY 1e-5 m
  The tolerance is set far below the error the navigation filter will carry. The
  OD error in the parking orbit has still to be studied and is expected to be of
  order 10 m, so a numerical error of 1e-6 m is about seven orders of magnitude
  below it. The propagator is therefore guaranteed not to contribute to the
  dispersion that block 2 of the plan exists to establish - the cloud of points at
  the start of the braking manoeuvre is navigation-limited by construction, not
  numerics-limited.

  Setting the tolerance this far down costs little: 32 s against 64 s is a factor
  of two in evaluations, and the whole arc is 4.3 s of wall clock.

================================================================================
5. LIMITS
================================================================================

  - RK4 only. Other integrators, fixed and variable step, are compared separately.
  - One orbit radius (35 km) and one orientation. Other altitudes are to be
    covered when the parking orbit itself is selected.
  - The step-halving estimate stops being meaningful below about 16 s on this arc.
    Certifying accuracy finer than ~1e-8 m would need an independent reference,
    for instance the Jacobi integral, which is exactly conserved on an unthrusted
    arc in the rotating frame and therefore needs no reference trajectory at all.
